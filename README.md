## The entry

> At twelve, crying over a lost race, my father said, 'You don't have to win — you have to finish,' and I still hear him every time I want to quit.

One sentence, 145 characters: a concrete scene (twelve years old, crying
over a lost race), a reversal (losing does not matter — finishing does), and
a landing line (still hearing it every time I want to quit).

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) validates the sentence — one line,
1–200 characters, a single sentence ending in a full stop, no leading or
trailing space, no control characters or lone surrogates — and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
